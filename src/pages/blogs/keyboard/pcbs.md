## No Solder assembly of Controller board to PCB

Alternative non-soldering or low-solder methods include:

- Press-fit headers: Using staggered header pins that press onto PCB pads with enough tension to make a connection, then only soldering the pins to the controller itself
- Spring-loaded headers: Also known as "Japanese spring headers," these offer a swappable connection but can be unreliable and expensive
- Dupont connectors: Using crimped wires with Dupont plugs, though this still requires soldering the wires to the PCB and controller
- Mill-Max sockets are widely recommended for their stability and low profile, whereas spring headers are often considered less robust for controller connections because each pin carries more critical signals than a single switch
    - [mechboards](https://mechboards.co.uk/products/mill-max-sockets?variant=40330077143245)
    - [mill-max site](https://www.mill-max.com/)
    - [Mill-Max Socket Guide](https://docs.keebd.com/information/mill-max-sockets)
