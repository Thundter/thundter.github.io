Exposure of deceptive practices within the UK supermarket yogurt industry, where many popular products are found to be highly processed "engineered" items rather than traditional yogurt

## Key Exposures and Issues

- Added Ingredients - Many yogurts use milk powder, water, whipping cream, and modified starch rather than simple fermented milk
- Dead Bacteria - Because many products are heat-treated after fermentation to extend shelf life, the beneficial live cultures are often dead by the time they reach the consumer
- Greek Style Myth - Many products labeled "Greek style" are simply regular yogurt thickened with added cream, starch, or thickeners instead of being properly strained
- Hidden Sugar - Some premium "Greek style" options contain more sugar than soft drinks, sometimes exceeding 17g per 100g
- The "Bovaer" Controversy - The source discusses concerns regarding a feed additive used in dairy trials to reduce methane emissions, highlighting that organic options are currently the safest way to avoid milk from these trials
